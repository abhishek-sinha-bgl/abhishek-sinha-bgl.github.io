About

Abhishek Sinha

Technology executive, independent researcher and writer exploring the changing relationship between AI, enterprise decisions and evidence.

Background

My career has been spent at the intersection of technology, business and organisational change. Over more than three decades, I have worked across enterprise architecture, global technology services, complex outsourcing relationships and transformation, including senior leadership roles at Hewlett-Packard, IBM and Kyndryl. The work has taken me across industries and international markets, often where technical choices, commercial commitments and organisational realities must be reconciled.

I currently serve as Global CTO at Arth Group. My perspective is shaped by the practical demands of making technology work in large organisations, rather than by technology alone. For a fuller career history, my LinkedIn profile provides the details.

Current interests

Much of my current work and writing concerns what happens when increasingly capable AI becomes part of everyday enterprise decisions and operations. I am interested in the relationship between AI capability and human judgment, the limits of governance and assurance, and the organisational and commercial changes that follow successful adoption. These questions extend to agentic systems, enterprise accountability, outsourcing and the wider supplier ecosystem.

Through Epistamate, I explore how research and advisory workflows can preserve the evidence behind conclusions, distinguish what is established from what remains uncertain, and respond when new information changes earlier findings. That includes work on research synthesis, regulatory intelligence and AI-enabled advisory processes. The emphasis is on making evidence and reasoning inspectable, not treating a persuasive AI-generated answer as proof.

I write about these questions here in essays, and more specifically about evidence-led AI systems through Epistamate Insights.
