Background

What I write about

The perspective here is operational, not theoretical. I write from direct experience across four decades of enterprise technology leadership — from the inside of large outsourcing deals, global delivery organisations, and AI governance implementation programmes. The topics I focus on are:

  • AI Governance & EU AI Act — What the regulation actually requires operationally, not just legally. What CIOs need to do now versus what they think they need to do.
  • GCC Strategy in India — Entry strategy, state incentives, captive vs outsourced decisions, talent scaling, and building Centres of Excellence that become critical to global delivery capability.
  • AIOps & Agentic AI — The gap between GenAI experimentation and production-grade Agentic AI. What good governance frameworks look like in regulated industries.
  • CIO as Commercial Leader — How technology decisions translate into EBITDA, NPS, and revenue growth. The CFO partnership. FinOps as strategy, not cost management.
  • Enterprise Outsourcing — What actually goes wrong in large outsourcing deals and how to prevent it. What $250M+ in cost gap elimination teaches you about contract design.
